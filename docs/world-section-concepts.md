# Selected direction: archive, equipment, departure

Selected with the owner on 2026-10-06. These are concepts for discussion, not implemented scenes. Keep the existing résumé data authoritative and the games independent of portfolio access.

## Experience: the archive corridor

A quiet corridor of numbered company doors, chronological from earliest role to current work. Approaching a door lights its company plaque and dates. E opens a compact scene with three evidence panels: role, work shipped, impact. The current company room is lit and active; earlier rooms feel like recorded chapters. Use optional drawers for longer details instead of putting the whole résumé in one modal. Every doorway remains accessible without playing a game.

## Tech stack: the equipment room

A preparation room with stations for frontend, backend, mobile, AI, and infrastructure. Select a tool to illuminate the projects that use it, showing the connection between technology and work. A short console explains what it was used for. Avoid fake skill percentages or equipment that claims experience absent from resume.ts. Keyboard/touch interactions use the existing E/button controls.

## Contact: the departure lounge

A calmer final room with an exit toward daylight. A desk phone opens the email action, a dossier opens the résumé, and two departure boards link to GitHub and LinkedIn. Keep explicit labels on every action and provide copy-email next to mailto. A marked exit returns to the simple portfolio. Visitors can reach contact directly through the map.

## Suggested next implementation order

1. Archive corridor: establish the common approach → inspect → close interaction.
2. Equipment room: connect tool choices to existing project data.
3. Departure lounge: finish with clear contact actions and a visible return route.

The current iteration implements the first-visit invitation animation and the two entry choices, and restores the original Mac/IDE portfolio at /portfolio per the owner’s follow-up. The three scenes above await the next implementation step.
